Recursive Box Puzzle Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026 · App: Recursive Box Puzzle (com.codeenzyme.recursiveboxpuzzle) · Developer: Code Enzyme

Recursive Box Puzzle is a block-pushing puzzle game for Android in which boxes hold whole rooms. This policy explains what information is handled when you play it, why, and the choices you have. The short version: the game has no accounts and never asks for your name or contact details. The one purchase goes through Google Play, play statistics go to Google Analytics where no consent is required for them, and the optional hint videos come from Google AdMob, which collects some device information to serve them.

Information the game stores on your device

Recursive Box Puzzle saves the levels you have solved and your best move counts, the level you are on, your hints, a record of whether you have unlocked the full game, and your sound and vibration settings in the app's private storage on your phone. This saved progress never leaves your device. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of its own.

Purchases (Google Play)

The full game is a single one-time purchase sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and Code Enzyme never sees your card or payment details. Google Play tells the game when the purchase is complete so it can unlock the levels, and keeps a record of the purchase on your Google account so it can be restored if you reinstall. Google's handling of purchases is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Play statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To learn which levels are too hard and how the game is played, Recursive Box Puzzle can send play statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google. They say which level was started or finished, how many moves and undos it took, how long it took, whether a hint video was watched, and whether the full game was unlocked. Google's software in the game adds when the game was first opened and how long it was played, and general information about the device, such as its model, system version, language and country, together with a random identifier for this installation of the game. The statistics do not contain your name, email address or contacts, and the game tells Google not to use them for advertising.

Play statistics are sent only where Google's consent check for the game reports that no consent is required. In particular, they stay switched off in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. Everywhere else you can switch them off at any time under Settings › Share play statistics in the game. Google's handling of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Recursive Box Puzzle can offer short video ads that you choose to watch in exchange for a hint. They are provided by Google AdMob and only play when you tap to watch one. There are no banners and no ads between levels. When a video loads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes information from your device, which may include:

Your choices

  • Consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, or another region where consent is required, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad loads. Until you have chosen, the game does not start the ad service at all. You can change your choice at any time under Settings › Privacy choices in the game.
  • Play statistics: switch them off under Settings › Share play statistics in the game. The switch is not shown where statistics are already off because consent would be required.
  • Skipping ads: hint videos are optional. If you never ask for one, no ad is shown.
  • Personalised ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android settings under Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Google › Ads on some phones).
  • Deleting your data: Settings › Reset progress in the game clears your solved levels, and uninstalling Recursive Box Puzzle deletes everything it stored on your device. For data held by Google for purchases, play statistics or advertising, see the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Recursive Box Puzzle is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
