Recursive Box Puzzle Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026 · App: Recursive Box Puzzle (com.codeenzyme.recursiveboxpuzzle) · Developer: Code Enzyme

Recursive Box Puzzle is a block-pushing puzzle game for Android in which boxes hold whole rooms. This policy explains what information is handled when you play it, why, and the choices you have. The short version: the game itself does not ask for, collect or send any personal information. The one purchase goes through Google Play, and the optional hint videos come from Google AdMob, which collects some device information to serve them.

Information the game stores on your device

Recursive Box Puzzle saves the levels you have solved and your best move counts, the level you are on, your hints, a record of whether you have unlocked the full game, and your sound and vibration settings in the app's private storage on your phone. This information never leaves your device. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no servers of its own.

Purchases (Google Play)

The full game is a single one-time purchase sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and Code Enzyme never sees your card or payment details. Google Play tells the game when the purchase is complete so it can unlock the levels, and keeps a record of the purchase on your Google account so it can be restored if you reinstall. Google's handling of purchases is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Recursive Box Puzzle can offer short video ads that you choose to watch in exchange for a hint. They are provided by Google AdMob and only play when you tap to watch one. There are no banners and no ads between levels. When a video loads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes information from your device, which may include:

This information is shared with Google, which acts as the advertising provider. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit.

Your choices

  • Consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, or another region where consent is required, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad loads. Until you have chosen, the game does not start the ad service at all. You can change your choice at any time under Settings › Privacy choices in the game.
  • Skipping ads: hint videos are optional. If you never ask for one, no ad is shown.
  • Personalised ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android settings under Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Google › Ads on some phones).
  • Deleting your data: Settings › Reset progress in the game clears your solved levels, and uninstalling Recursive Box Puzzle deletes everything it stored on your device. For data held by Google for purchases or advertising, see the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Recursive Box Puzzle is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
